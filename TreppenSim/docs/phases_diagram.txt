TreppenSchlepper — Step Climbing Phases (side view, to scale)
=============================================================
Components:  OS = OuterShell (250×140 mm)     IS = SlidingInnerShell (250×80 mm)
             FP = FootPlate (250×18 mm)        EC = ElevatorCylinder (stroke 180 mm)
             HC = HorizontalCylinder (stroke 250 mm, body 120 mm)
             o  = wheel (Ø 70 mm, axis 28 mm above OS lower edge → 7 mm clearance)

Stair geometry (18/27):  rise = 180 mm,  tread depth = 270 mm
Config B:  FP carries battery + MCU + sensors (8 kg);  OS is structure only (9 kg)
Payload: 20 kg on OS.  Total moving mass Phase 2: 36 kg.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 PHASE 1 — APPROACH  (wheels drive; tank steering via differential rotation)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    ╔═══════════════════════════════════════╗
    ║              payload                  ║
    ╟[HC━━━━━━━━]  ←contracted,             ║   HC sits in 60 mm gap between
    ╟──┬──────────────────────────────────╢ ║   IS ceiling and OS ceiling;
    ║  │   IS         [EC retracted]      ║ ║   rod tip connects to IS ceiling
    ║  │  [FP: batt+MCU+sensors]          ║ ║   via bracket at IS rear face
    ╚══╧══════════════════════════════════╝ ║
   o  7mm gap (OS bottom above tread)     o ╝
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────    ┌──────────────
                   lower tread / ground                        │  upper tread
                                                     (rise 180 mm)
 Note: IS+FP+EC fully retracted inside OS.
       Wheel axes at 28 mm above OS lower edge.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 PHASE 2 — ELEVATION  (EC extends, lifting IS + OS + payload together)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    ╔════════════════════════════════════╗
    ║             payload               ║   IS + OS elevated together
    ╟[HC━━━━━━━━] contracted            ║   to upper-tread height (+7 mm)
    ╟──┬──────────────────────────────╢ ║
    ║  │   IS      [EC↑↑ extending]  ║ ║                         ┌──────────
   o║  │                             ║ ║o  ─────────────────────  │upper tread
    ╚══╧═════════════════════════════╝ ║   ← 7 mm clearance above tread
       │         ↑↑ EC ↑↑             │
       │  IS anchor (rigid during P2) │   EC spans FP top → IS ceiling
       │                              │   (total height ≈ 240 mm extended)
 ──────┴──────────[FP]────────────────┴─────────────────────────
               lower tread  ← FP stays anchored here
               FP = reaction point for EC

 Peak EC force: ~391 N.   Phase 2 duration: ~4.1 s.   Energy: ~70 J.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 PHASE 3 — SLIDE FORWARD  (HC extends, pushing OS over upper tread)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

                   ╔════════════════════════════════════╗
    ┌────────────┐ ║             payload                ║
    │            ├─╢bracket                             ║   OS advanced 250 mm
    │     IS     │ ║[━━━━━━━━━HC rod━━━━━━━━━HC body━━]║   over upper step
    │   [EC↑↑]   │ ╚════════════════════════════════════╝
    │   [FP]     │                  o                  o
 ───┴────────────┴───      ─────────────────────────────────────
        lower tread                     upper tread
   (IS + FP anchored here)
   IS = HC reaction point

 HC extends left-to-right: rod tip fixed at IS ceiling (left),
 body fixed to OS rear (right).  Front wheel crosses step edge at t ≈ 0.29 s.
 Min stability margin: +9.0 cm (device never tips for default params).
 Peak HC force: ~49 N.   Phase 3 duration: ~1.0 s.   Energy: ~2.8 J.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 PHASE 4 — RETRACT  (FP freed; HC retracts pulling IS+FP back under OS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    ╔════════════════════════════════════╗
    ║              payload              ║   OS fully on upper tread
    ╟[HC━━━━━━━━] ←contracting         ║   HC pulls IS+FP back inside OS
    ╟──┬──────────────────────────────╢ ║   EC retracts simultaneously
    ║  │   IS    [EC↓ retracting]    ║ ║   (lowers IS to OS floor height)
    ║  │  [FP: batt+MCU+sensors]     ║ ║
    ╚══╧══════════════════════════════╝ ║
   o  7mm gap                         o ╝
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                    upper tread  ← device fully here, ready for next step

 Peak EC force (P4): ~193 N.   Phase 4 duration: ~1.0 s.   Energy: ~32 J.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 SUMMARY — one full step cycle  (18/27 stair, 20 kg payload, Config B)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

 Phase 1  ──wheels──►  Position in front of step           (variable duration)

 Phase 2  ──EC↑──►    Lift IS+OS+payload to upper height   ~4.1 s   ~70 J
                       FP anchored on lower tread
                       Peak EC force: 391 N  (LINAK LA12 750 N rated ✓)

 Phase 3  ──HC→──►    Push OS forward 250 mm over step     ~1.0 s    ~3 J
                       IS+FP anchored; stability margin +9 cm throughout
                       Peak HC force: 49 N  (requires rack/belt drive for 350 mm/s)

 Phase 4  ──HC←──►    Retract IS+FP into OS; EC retracts   ~1.0 s   ~32 J
                       FP lifted off lower tread first

                       ────────────────────────────────────────────
                       Total active time per step:   ~6.1 s
                       Total energy per step:        ~105 J
                       ────────────────────────────────────────────
                       ▶ Repeat for next step
